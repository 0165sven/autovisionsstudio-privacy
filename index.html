AutoVisionsStudio Content Distribution – Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for the internal AutoVisionsStudio content distribution application.

Overview

AutoVisionsStudio Content Distribution is an internal automation application used by AutoVisionsStudio to manage content and related business activity on our own Pinterest Business account. The application is not offered as a public service to third-party users.

Purpose of the application

The application connects our internal n8n automation workflows with Pinterest. It may be used to create, schedule, manage, and analyze Pins and boards, link Pins to AutoVisionsStudio product listings on Etsy, and, where separately enabled, support advertising or commerce-related workflows for our own business account.

Information the application may access

Depending on the permissions granted through Pinterest OAuth, the application may access information associated with our own Pinterest Business account, including account identifiers, boards, Pins, Pin metadata, performance and analytics data, and advertising or campaign information where such access is enabled. The application may also use product information, product images, and public Etsy listing URLs from AutoVisionsStudio.

How we use information

We use this information only to operate and improve AutoVisionsStudio's own content distribution, publishing, reporting, analytics, and advertising workflows. We may analyze Pin performance to improve future content decisions. The application does not require Pinterest data from unrelated third-party users for its intended operation.

Customer information

The Pinterest publishing workflow is designed to use product and listing information rather than Etsy customer personal data. Customer names, addresses, payment information, or private order details are not required for creating or managing Pinterest Pins.

Sharing of information

AutoVisionsStudio does not sell Pinterest account data or personal information. Information is not shared with third parties for their independent marketing purposes. Data may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the automation environment, such as Pinterest, Google, Etsy, n8n, and infrastructure providers, subject to their respective terms and privacy practices.

Storage and security

Authentication credentials, access tokens, identifiers, and workflow data are stored only as needed to operate the application and are protected within the automation environment using reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Access is limited to the operator of AutoVisionsStudio and authorized services required for the workflow.

Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the application or to meet applicable legal obligations. Data that is no longer required may be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

Your choices and access revocation

Pinterest access is authorized through Pinterest's OAuth process. Access can be revoked through the relevant Pinterest account settings or by disconnecting the application. Revoking access prevents the application from making future authorized requests to the Pinterest account.

Children's privacy

The application is intended solely for business use and is not directed to children.

Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the application's functionality, data use, or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available at this public page.
